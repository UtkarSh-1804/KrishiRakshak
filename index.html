How this prototype works (every part explained)

What each part does

  1. Farm weather button: asks the browser for location only after you press it. The backend reverse-geocodes coordinates into locality, state and country, then finds the nearest mapped IMD city station and requests its official forecast. Station and observation timestamp are shown where available. IMD credentials are required; no sample values are presented as live weather.
  2. Weather panel: shows IMD temperature, humidity, rainfall recorded in the last 24 hours and wind speed when present. A missing metric is shown as unavailable, never invented.
  3. Crop photo: the browser previews your photo locally; it is not uploaded. The demo backend uses your selected crop, symptoms and weather in transparent rules. It does not run AI image recognition.
  4. Early warning: humidity can add a scouting reminder. Weather is a risk cue, never proof of disease.
  5. QR scanner: a bundled JavaScript QR decoder reads camera frames and uploaded QR images without relying on browser-specific scanner support. Manual code entry also works. Scanning happens in your browser; the resulting code is used to look up the small demo catalog.
  6. Product advice: the catalog displays expiry, target and safety reminders. It intentionally avoids chemical doses because labels differ by formulation, crop and jurisdiction. QR data does not establish authenticity.
  7. Backend: server.py uses Python's standard library to serve this page and provide /api/weather, /api/reverse-geocode, /api/diagnose, and /api/product/<code>.

For a real SIH deployment: replace demo catalog and symptom rules with verified ICAR/state agriculture data, an evaluated crop-specific vision model, Marathi localization, expert review and secure farm data handling.
